Privacy.

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Polarity ("the game") is published by SoftPin s.r.o. ("we"). Contact: support@softpin.space.

What the game stores on your device

Your settings, scores, game history, achievements, coins, helper balances, unlocked themes and any unfinished game are stored on your device. Deleting the game deletes them, unless they are also in your cloud save (below).

Cloud save

If you are signed in to Google Play Games (Android) or Game Center (iPhone), the game keeps a copy of your progress in your own saved games there, so it follows you to a new phone: coins, helpers, best scores, level stars, statistics, your most recent games, achievements, Daily Board records, your daily streak, event badges, the weekly reward, the themes you unlocked with coins, ads or your streak, your settings, and whether you bought Premium or the Void theme, so they work on your other devices without "Restore purchases". An unfinished game stays on the device it is played on. If more than one account signs in on the same device, each account's progress is kept apart on the device and goes only to that account's saved games. The saved game is stored by Google or Apple under their privacy policies, in your account. We cannot read it. You can delete it from Play Games or from iCloud settings, and "Reset all data" in the game replaces it with a fresh start.

Play statistics

To learn how the game is played and make it better, the game sends anonymous play statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase. They include:

  • game events: runs started and ended (mode, board size, layer reached, score, length), levels started, won, lost or left, Daily Board results, boss layers, perks picked, helpers used, rewarded ads watched and where, themes unlocked, weekly rewards, purchases delivered, tutorial steps
  • device and app information: device model, operating system and app version, language, and approximate country, derived from your IP address
  • an identifier Firebase creates for this installation of the game

They do not include your name, email address, player name or contacts, and they are not used for advertising. Google processes them for us as a service provider. They are kept for up to 14 months and then deleted.

You can turn this off at any time in Settings, "Share usage statistics". Nothing more is sent after that.

Error reports

If the game runs into an error while "Share usage statistics" is on, it sends us a report so we can find and fix it. It holds:

  • the error and where in the game's code it happened
  • the app version, the operating system and its version, and the language
  • an identifier created for this installation of the game, so reports from one phone can be told apart
  • the game you were playing at the time (the board, the score, the layer) and the last things you did in the game, such as "started a Classic run" or "swiped left"

It holds no name, email address or contacts. Reports are stored in our Firebase project (Google Cloud Firestore) and deleted after 90 days.

Feedback

"Send feedback" in Settings sends us your message and the kind you picked (a problem, an idea or something else), with the app version, the operating system and the language. It also holds your email address if you give one, so we can answer, and, if you leave "Attach game info" on, the same game details as an error report. We use it only to answer you and improve the game. It is stored in our Firebase project and deleted after a year.

Game settings

The game downloads its settings (prices, rewards and difficulty, for example) from Firebase Remote Config, so we can adjust them without an update. For this, Google receives your IP address and the identifier Firebase creates for this installation. Some players may get slightly different settings, to test which work better.

Advertising

The game shows ads only when you ask for one: a rewarded video you choose to watch for a free helper, coins, a revive or a Daily Board try. Nothing interrupts a game. Ads are served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)
  • your approximate location from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and tap

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is requested, through Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, "Ad privacy choices". On iOS the game asks for permission to track through Apple's prompt; if you decline, you still receive ads, but they are not personalised. You can also reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings.

Google's use of this data is described at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Premium gives another try without its ad (a revive, a helper that ran out, a Daily Board try). The ads that pay coins stay ads you can choose to watch, with Premium too.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by the App Store or Google Play. We receive only a confirmation of what was bought, used to unlock it on your device. We do not receive your payment details.

Game Center and Google Play Games

If you are signed in, your Classic scores, your Daily Board score for the day and your player name are sent to Apple Game Center or Google Play Games to show leaderboards, and your progress is kept in your saved games there (see Cloud save). The game also reads your place in this week's ranking from there, to pay the weekly reward. This is handled under Apple's and Google's own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device. No data leaves the device for this.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn off play statistics and error reports in Settings, "Share usage statistics", and ad personalisation in "Ad privacy choices". To have your feedback or error reports deleted sooner, or to ask what we hold, write to support@softpin.space.

Changes

We will post changes to this policy here and update the date above.
