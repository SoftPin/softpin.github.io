01 / 04How it plays

Every tile
has a charge.

+4 merges with +4 into +8. But +4 meets −4 and both vanish in a burst. Easy to learn, deep to master.

01

Swipe

One swipe slides every tile. Same sign merges, opposite signs cancel. Cancels score more than merges and free two cells at once.

02

Chain

Keep colliding to climb the chain from ×1 to ×5, then hold it to Overcharge and multiply every point.

03

Plan

See the Next tile before it drops. When you need a hand, use Undo, Remix or Boost.

02 / 04Ways to play

Play it
your way.

Short hand-made puzzles, an endless board, or one new puzzle for everyone each day.

LEVELS

50 levels, 10 bosses

Hand-made puzzles with three stars to earn on each. Bosses freeze, eat, glitch and mirror your tiles, and each plays by its own rules.

CLASSIC

Endless board

4×4, 5×5 and 6×6 boards. Each layer brings bigger tiles, then stones, black cubes and frozen tiles. All-time and weekly leaderboards.

DAILY

A new board every day

The same puzzle for every player, with three stars to win. Daily goals, a quest chest and streak rewards.

THEMES

18 themes

Collect looks from neon nights and the deep sea to candy skies. Colorblind shapes, reduced motion and a left-handed layout are built in.

04 / 04Questions
Is Polarity free?

Yes. You can play the whole game for free. Optional purchases (Premium, coin packs and a theme) are sold through Google Play.

Are there ads?

An ad only plays when you choose one, for a reward such as free coins or an extra try. Nothing interrupts a game.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The game plays offline, with no timers. Leaderboards, cloud save and ad rewards need a connection.

Can I move to a new phone?

Yes. Sign in to Google Play Games and your progress follows you through cloud save.

Which devices are supported?

Polarity runs on recent Android phones and tablets. An iPhone and iPad version is planned. If it does not run on your device, tell us which model and OS version you use.

What data does the game collect?

Only what is described in our Privacy Policy. Play statistics can be turned off in Settings.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We would love to hear it. Use Settings, Send feedback in the game, visit Support or email hello@softpin.space.
